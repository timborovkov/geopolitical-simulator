# Initial content pack: 1 January 2000

The default `initial-2000-interest-accrual.json` uses **content schema 17 / rules 17**, ID `initial-2000-interest-accrual-v17`. It preserves the preceding roster and subsystems, adding separately authored fixed 4% actual/365 principal-day accrual. Fractional reporting units carry without compounding until whole interest becomes due; whole dues reconcile separately to actual cash payments and external arrears. The noncash fractional creditor claim survives principal retirement. It never funds spending. Treasury and forecasts distinguish mean rates, exact interval dues and actually paid cash. Tax, wage, purchase and price precision remain whole reporting units. This is a gameplay accounting convention, not historical debt pricing or legal settlement law.

The preserved `initial-2000-cash-management.json` uses **content schema 16 / rules 16**, ID `initial-2000-cash-management-v16`. Future targets are not money, and zero collected receipts cannot fund a target. This is an authored gameplay procedure, not historical cash-management law.

## Authored models and units

**All remaining economic values and coefficients are authored gameplay approximations**, including debt, cash, taxes, spending, capacities, group profiles, political approval, council thresholds, marker coordinates and ideology profiles. The source ID `authored-balance-v1` identifies the original calibration; new household/government definitions are original scenario content. Historical observations initialize the game; they are not causal coefficients.

Money uses millions of constant-2000 USD-equivalent game credits. Currency codes are identity metadata, not exchange-rate behavior. Annual GDP is an opening calibration, not a measured evolving national account. Population uses integer persons. Daily food/energy use modeled units per thousand persons, not empirical tonnes/GWh. Rates use integer percentage points; program capital costs use basis points of opening GDP. Positive divisions truncate; largest-remainder allocation preserves exact group totals and breaks ties by stable definition order. Small economies have visible rounding effects.

Food and energy have distinct nonnegative Rust quantity types across stocks and physical flows. Checked arithmetic rejects overflow and invalid ratios; rational scaling uses a wide intermediate and rounds down. Their JSON representation remains an integer, preserving version-2 snapshots and canonical replay results. Rates, distances and time still require further dimensional typing.

Four disjoint household classifications partition population and cash: working, public-service, transfer-dependent and asset-owning households. Population, income, savings and working-age shares are authored, not measured census or income-distribution observations. Public-service payments go to the public-service group; welfare goes 80% to transfer-dependent and 20% to working households. Private income follows authored shares. Group taxes, purchases, food delivery, employment and arrears are inspectable. Regions, age transitions, skills, complete labor markets and an endogenous price system are not yet implemented.

Living-standard indicators are read-only calculations over these disjoint group records. Income below the essential basket is financial pressure and can be offset by saved cash; actual food delivery is separate. Cash/basket days assume today's basket cost and ignore future income. The Gini is population-weighted from group-mean disposable income, rounded down to basis points; it excludes within-group inequality and is undefined for zero total income, negative disposable group income or income assigned to a zero-person group. These are authored-model outcomes, not survey poverty or inequality estimates. Before the first settlement, clients withhold realized indicators. No authoritative save state or accounting rule changes for these queries.

The external market is a separate counterpart per country, not a network between selectable states. It starts with three times opening GDP in cash, supplies up to 25% of energy needs and 35% of food needs, buys service exports worth up to 15% of realized output and lends subject to cash and debt limits. It can exhaust its funds. This is bounded trade/lending, not complete international finance.

Government definitions specify decision route, backing threshold, consent delay, council threshold and optional leadership-review interval. The UK reference uses 51 parliamentary support and 14-day consent; China uses elite/cadre backing 55/50 and 14-day consent; Saudi Arabia uses elite/council backing 50/45, immediate decrees and a council condition on large spending increases. These and their review intervals are authored institutional mechanisms, not exact constitutional reproductions. See [governing-term scope](../docs/initial-game.md) for consent, reservation, election and succession rules.

The selectable fiscal/supply crisis overrides only the selected country's policy/capacity/approval calibration; it retains the same population/GDP observations. Its scenario ID is saved and replayed. Seven programs cover income tax, services, welfare, energy, food, administration and support negotiations. Metadata specifies parameter bounds, base delays, capital costs and affected groups; Rust owns behavior. Unknown policy behavior is rejected.

## Dated identities and bookmarks

Content schema 2 now accepts optional `identities` and country `governing_office` fields. The catalogue contains stable typed person/office IDs, office roles and existence intervals, office-holder terms and primary-currency intervals. Every record includes a source ID, source version and an explicit quality class: observed, reconciled, interpolated, inferred, authored or unknown. Names can change independently of IDs. Reference office roles do not grant command powers; the existing government configuration still defines playable authority.

Intervals include `valid_from` and exclude `valid_to`; a null end is open-ended. Terms cannot precede or outlast their office's existence. Overlapping incumbencies, conflicting primary currencies, missing references, duplicate IDs/sources and invalid dates fail assembly. Collective holders list explicit members; vacancy is recorded separately from an optional office with no known incumbent. A required active office must have a person, collective or explicit vacancy. This does not yet implement full appointment/succession law, personnel AI, country founding/dissolution, memberships or territorial transitions.

The loader assembles the governing name/title and currency for `Content.start`. `Content::at_bookmark` recompiles those fields for another supported date and validates the result. A campaign branches from this assembly: later reference terms and currency changes are not automatically enacted. Save content hashes prevent silently replacing a campaign's starting records. Optional fields are omitted for the original pack, preserving its rules-2 world checksum and canonical world encoding.

Civil reference dates use the Gregorian calendar, years 0001–9999. Campaign starts range from 2000-01-01 through 2095-01-01, allowing a five-calendar-year review by the existing 2100-01-01 limit. February 29 anniversaries use February 28 in a non-leap year. Ticks retain the 2000 epoch; later starts initialize fiscal execution at the bookmark through the next January boundary and offset authored election/leadership timers. Reporting and AI reviews retain their authored 30-day cadence. Annual calibration observations accept years up to the bookmark year; exact publication-date visibility and full time-series assembly remain incomplete.

Settlement uses yesterday's announced index. Food reprices the configured part of household baskets, and both prices scale bounded import bills. Supply versus affordable demand determines the following day's index, with capped proportional adjustments and downward integer rounding. Physical need, requested use and delivered consumption remain distinct. Food requests reflect household affordability; energy requests are physical industrial need, with cash settlement modeled for imports only. Price changes neither create money nor rewrite earlier transactions. The record reconciles opening stock plus production/imports to delivered use, explicit loss and closing stock. Existing supply, fiscal, consent, political and AI phases remain ordered; full sector recipes, wage-setting, general inflation and banking remain absent.

Rules-3 snapshots require format 4; rules-2 snapshots continue to write format 3. Supported older snapshots resolve to the classic bundled data when no explicit pack is pinned and retain their original files until explicitly saved/restored. A converted recovery backup is uniquely archived before later backup rotation can overwrite it. Save conversion archives originals by their format number; incompatible rules or content are rejected rather than repricing an old campaign. Autosave and default manual filenames distinguish formats.
