# Eversim reference inventory

Research date: 2 November 2026. These are advertised capabilities, not independently tested implementations. Feature labels below are deliberately compact; they are a research checklist for an independently designed game, not copied implementation specifications.

## Coverage and limits

Reviewed: Steam descriptions for Power & Revolution 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023; Geo-Political Simulator 5 and 2026; God’n Spy and Modding Tool pages for 2023, GPS5 and GPS2026; 2016–2019 scenario packs sold for the 2020 edition; current official simulation specification; 2016 manual introduction/contents; the visible GPS2026 announcement feed; official 2027 feature announcement.

**This is not an exhaustive enumeration of every internal policy, data field, character, scenario or historical Steam announcement.** Store pages themselves say “over” and “etc.”; they do not publish the complete database. The announcement feed exposes ten posts through April 2026 and has a dynamic “See More Content” boundary. Older announcement pagination, earlier GPS1–3 products, original 2016 base-game Steam page, every historical DLC reissue, full manuals for every edition, and in-game menus have not been audited. No game was installed or played. A later hands-on menu audit is required before claiming feature completeness.

Current scope interpretation: the proposed game’s player controls a government/country. Opposition, corporations, cartels, wealthy individuals and other actors remain simulation counterparts; standalone CEO/opposition careers are reference ideas rather than launch obligations.

## Baseline world-model inventory

The official simulation specification advertises about 175 playable nations within 230 modeled countries/territories; 600+ variables per sovereign state; 1,200+ administrative areas; 7,000+ cities; 1,000+ military bases; ports, airports, highways, railways, bridges, tunnels, pipelines, wells, power facilities and heritage sites. These are vendor counts, not our verified records.

Domain coverage: geography, climate, hazards, forests; age structure, longevity, contraception and migration; GDP, income, unemployment, inflation, growth, HDI; production/consumption, energy reserves, tourism and trade; public budgets, debt, ratings, taxes, currencies, interest rates and equities; wages, benefits, pensions, working hours, housing and healthcare; reproductive rights, marriage/adoption, euthanasia, prostitution and cannabis; education/research; constitutions, elections, parties and legislatures; policing, prisons, capital punishment, road safety; religious groups, sects, terrorism, armed forces, conscription; culture, internet and sports.

Characters have age, health, competence, ambition, affiliation, opinions, influence and other traits. The specification claims 50,000+ characters, 7,000+ firms, detailed records for 3,000 firms, 130+ sectors and 100+ research advances. [Official simulation specification](https://www.geo-political-simulator-5.com/simulation.php?langue=en)

The older official specification names UN bodies, IMF, World Bank, NATO, G7, OPEC, EU, CIS, MERCOSUR, NAFTA, Arab League and NGOs within 50+ organizations; those names reflect historical editions, not a verified 2000 or 2026 membership list. It also identifies organization meetings, elections and sporting calendars as scenario inputs. ## Translation into our project: design judgments

1. Preserve the breadth as a tagged backlog, but ship a coherent government game first. Corporate/opposition/personal systems should initially serve consequences and counterparty decisions.
2. Make causal inspection a normal feature. Separate legitimate player intelligence from developer omniscience; do not conceal basic economic explanations behind a paid cheat tool.
3. Turn recurring defect categories into invariant tests: finite numbers, conservation of goods/money, bounded capacities, stable IDs, valid paths, atomic treaties, reproducible votes and save/reload equivalence.
4. Build one policy/actor/event model reused by scenario authoring, AI and player commands. Country-specific institutions need data-driven rules rather than duplicated national code.
5. Do not import a current-world dataset into a 2000 start unchanged. Parties, leaders, borders, technologies, organizations and memberships must be valid at the scenario date.
6. Proposed scope is not equivalent to verified simulation accuracy. Each subsystem needs an explicit abstraction, player decisions, feedback, testable outcomes and limits.
